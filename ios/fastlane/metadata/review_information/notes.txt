=== YaruNavi - App Review Notes ===

No sign-in or account is required; all task data is stored locally. The AI
feature needs an internet connection (requests go through a Cloudflare Worker
proxy to the Anthropic API). The app's default language is Japanese; key
on-screen labels are quoted below with an English translation in parentheses.

----------------------------------------------------------------
1) ADD TASKS (needed before AI Sort)
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Launch the app to reach the Home screen.
2. Tap the round "+" button in the center of the bottom bar; a menu appears.
3. Tap the first item "タスクを追加" (Add task), enter a title and a due
   date, then save.
4. Add 3-5 tasks with different due dates.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2) RUN AI SORT (the main feature)
----------------------------------------------------------------
The large card at the top of the Home screen is the AI Sort card; its button
reads "今日のタスクをAIで整理" (Sort today's tasks with AI). Tap it (internet
required). The AI proposes an execution date and a priority for each task,
then opens the result screen.

----------------------------------------------------------------
3) IN-APP PURCHASES
----------------------------------------------------------------
Free users get 2 AI Sorts total (lifetime). On the 3rd attempt, a purchase
sheet titled "AI 整理の回数が不足しています" (Not enough AI sorts left) opens
with three options:
  - "プレミアムプラン ¥580/月" (Premium - 580 yen/month; 4,200 yen/year is
    also offered on the store screen).
    Products: yarunavi_premium_monthly / yarunavi_premium_yearly.
  - "AI 整理チケット ¥120" (AI Sort Ticket - 120 yen consumable, up to 3).
    Product: yarunavi_ai_ticket.
  - "動画を視聴" (Watch a video - free rewarded ad, up to 2 times total).

Quick path: run AI Sort twice to use the free quota; on the 3rd attempt the
sheet opens automatically. Tapping the ticket starts the StoreKit purchase;
tapping Premium opens the subscription store screen.

----------------------------------------------------------------
4) PREMIUM FROM SETTINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Open the "設定" (Settings) tab (rightmost icon) and tap "プレミアムに登録"
(Upgrade to Premium) to open the store screen. It shows the monthly (580 yen)
and yearly (4,200 yen) plans, the auto-renewal notice, a Restore Purchases
button, and Terms / Privacy links. Premium includes 30 AI Sorts per month,
automatic notification scheduling, calendar sync, and an ad-free experience.

----------------------------------------------------------------
5) CALENDAR SYNC
----------------------------------------------------------------
Open the "カレンダー" (Calendar) tab (2nd icon from the left) and tap "取込"
(Import) at the top-right. This directly presents the iOS system calendar-
permission dialog (no custom pre-prompt). After you allow access, events for
the next 60 days are imported.

----------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------
- All data is stored locally; there is no sign-in and no account.
- Gamification (XP / Level / Streak) is cosmetic only and does not affect
  app functionality.
