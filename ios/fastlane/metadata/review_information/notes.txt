=== YaruNavi - App Review Notes ===

No sign-in or account is required; all task data is stored locally on the
device. The AI Sort feature needs an internet connection (requests go
through a Cloudflare Worker proxy to the Anthropic API). The app's
default language is Japanese; on-screen labels are quoted below with an
English translation in parentheses.

The AI Sort button is ALWAYS visible inside the large card at the TOP of
the Home screen as long as there is at least 1 task (any due date is
fine; today / this week / future are all OK). It works the same on
iPhone and iPad.

----------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO TEST AI SORT AND IN-APP PURCHASES
----------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: Add tasks
  1. On the Home tab (house icon, bottom-left), tap the round "+" button
     in the center of the bottom bar.
  2. The Add Task sheet opens directly. Enter a title and a due date
     (today, tomorrow, next week - any date is fine), then save.
  3. Add at least 3 tasks. They will appear under "今すぐ / 今週 /
     来週以降" sections.

STEP 2: Find the AI Sort button
  Return to the Home tab. At the TOP of the screen, you will see a
  LARGE DARK CARD that shows:
    - "TODAY" pill, streak (flame), and your level (Lv. + XP).
    - "今日の進捗" (Today's progress) count + ring.
    - A BRIGHT BUTTON inside the card labelled "タスクをAIで整理"
      (Sort tasks with AI).
  This button is ALWAYS visible inside the top card whenever any task
  exists (regardless of due date). If you don't see it, scroll UP to the
  very top of the Home screen.

STEP 3: Run AI Sort
  Tap "タスクをAIで整理" inside the top card. A loading dialog appears
  ("整理中…"). After ~5-10 seconds (internet required) the result
  screen opens with the AI-recommended execution date and priority for
  each task.

STEP 4: Reach the In-App Purchase sheet
  The app grants 2 free AI Sorts per lifetime. Use them up by tapping
  "タスクをAIで整理" twice. On the 3rd attempt, a bottom sheet titled
  "AI 整理の回数が不足しています" (Not enough AI sorts left) opens with
  three options:
    - Premium Plan: 580 JPY / month or 4,200 JPY / year
      Products: yarunavi_premium_monthly / yarunavi_premium_yearly
    - AI Sort Ticket: 120 JPY consumable (up to 3 lifetime purchases)
      Product: yarunavi_ai_ticket
    - "動画を視聴" Watch rewarded video (free, up to 2 lifetime uses)
  Tap a paid option to start the StoreKit purchase flow.

STEP 5: Premium subscription screen
  Open the "設定" (Settings) tab (rightmost icon in the bottom bar).
  Tap "プレミアムに登録" (Upgrade to Premium) to open the store screen.
  It shows monthly (580 JPY) and yearly (4,200 JPY) plans with an
  auto-renewal notice, a Restore Purchases button, and Terms / Privacy
  links. Premium includes 30 AI Sorts per month, automatic notification
  scheduling, calendar sync, and an ad-free experience.

----------------------------------------------------------------
CALENDAR SYNC (optional)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Open the "カレンダー" (Calendar) tab (2nd icon from the left). Tap the
round "+" button at the bottom and choose "カレンダーから取り込み"
(Import from calendar), or open the import action at the top. iOS
shows the system calendar-permission dialog directly (no custom
pre-prompt, per Guideline 5.1.1(iv)). After permission, events for the
next 60 days are imported.

----------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------
- All task data is stored locally; no sign-in and no account.
- Gamification (XP / Level / Streak) is cosmetic only.
- The proxy only forwards AI Sort requests; no personal data leaves
  the device.
