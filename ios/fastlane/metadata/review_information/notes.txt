This app uses AI to organize task priorities and suggest optimal execution dates.

No sign-in required. All data is stored locally on the device.
AI feature requires internet connection (proxied through Cloudflare Worker to Anthropic API).

Free users: 2 lifetime AI sorts + rewarded ad for 1 additional sort per day.
Premium: 30 AI sorts/month, auto notifications, calendar sync, ad-free.

To test AI sorting:
1. Add several tasks with different due dates
2. Tap "AIで整理" (AI Sort) button on home screen
3. View results with recommended execution dates

Gamification (XP/Level/Streak) is cosmetic only and does not affect app functionality.
